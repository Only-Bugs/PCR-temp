Verde

Verde helps urban professionals translate awareness into action — tracking daily choices like transport, meals, and energy use to reveal their real-world climate impact. It turns sustainability into a visual, measurable experience.

Download the App

About Verde

Verde bridges the gap between climate intention and everyday behaviour. By visualising emissions data as growth — your evolving “carbon persona” — it keeps sustainability engaging through gamified milestones, challenges, and community rewards.

Who It’s For

For digital-first professionals who care about the planet but crave simple, beautiful tools to act consistently — Verde offers a personal sustainability journey that’s measurable, social, and rewarding.
