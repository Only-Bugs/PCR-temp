About the app

Sustainability that feels personal and actionable

Verde translates everyday choices into an evolving carbon persona, giving you a vivid snapshot of your progress. Stay motivated with timely nudges, shared challenges, and insights that fit comfortably into a busy routine.

Key Features

Verde combines playful visuals with practical metrics so you can see the difference every ride, meal, and habit makes — and celebrate the wins along the way.

Personalised Carbon Persona

Watch your tree-ring persona grow with every sustainable action, turning impact into a story you can share.

Actionable Insights

Receive tailored recommendations that align with how you commute, eat, and live — no guilt trips, just smart nudges.

Community Challenges

Join collaborative missions and monthly challenges to keep the whole community inspired and accountable.
